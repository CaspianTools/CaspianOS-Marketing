Human Resources

The employee record other modules build on — who works here, what they are qualified to do, and when they are available.

  • Personnel files with departments, job titles, documents and emergency contacts
  • Leave requests with approval, balances and a team calendar
  • Training modules, sessions, attendance and a live competency matrix
  • QR-code attendance capture for sessions and toolbox talks

HSEQ

Health, safety, environment and quality — the module most customers arrive for, and the one that has to survive an audit.

  • Risk assessments with hazards, controls and residual risk scoring
  • Observations and near-misses raised from the field in seconds
  • Incident reporting that escalates into a full investigation with corrective actions
  • Permits to work and lifting plans with multi-step approval and a kanban board
  • Controlled documents and medical records under strict permissions

Business

Where strategy meets delivery: the KPIs the board watches and the projects that move them.

  • KPIs with targets, thresholds and an on-track / at-risk / off-track view
  • Projects with teams, phases, milestones and linked tasks
  • Every KPI and project is directly linkable — bookmark it, share it, open it

Efficiency

The layer people live in all day — and the reason they log in even on a quiet week.

  • Tasks with priorities, due dates, assignees and a board view
  • Meetings with agendas, attendance and actions that become tasks
  • Custom forms with shareable public links for people outside the system
  • Checklists, notes, a personal diary and a lean improvement register

Procurement

The full source-to-pay chain, with the approvals and paperwork your finance team already asks for.

  • Requisitions → purchase orders → goods receipts → vendor invoices
  • Vendor register with contracts, scorecards and performance history
  • Contractor personnel tracked with the same competency rules as employees
  • A shared item catalogue that inventory, manufacturing and finance all use

Inventory

Multi-warehouse stock control that keeps the system honest about what is actually on the shelf.

  • Stock by warehouse and zone, with reorder points and low-stock alerts
  • Lot and batch tracking with expiry dates
  • Transfer orders between sites and audited stock adjustments
  • Cycle counting by plan and session instead of one painful annual count

Manufacturing New

Turn raw material into finished goods without leaving the platform — and without a second inventory system.

  • Work orders scheduled against production lines and capacity
  • Multi-level bills of materials built from the shared item catalogue
  • Quality control checks recorded against the batch that failed them

Tools included

Maintenance

A CMMS for the equipment your operation depends on — reactive requests and preventive plans in one register.

  • Asset register with hierarchy, criticality, documents and history
  • Preventive plans triggered by calendar or meter reading
  • Calibration records with due dates, certificates and expiry warnings
  • Failure codes and warranty claims to make downtime analysable

CRM

The revenue side of the same platform — so a won deal becomes a sales order, not an email to operations.

  • Contacts and accounts with the full activity history
  • A drag-and-drop deal pipeline, plus forecast and win-rate analytics
  • Quotes that convert to sales orders and on to invoices

Finance

Receivables, payables and budgets sitting on the same records as the operation that generated them.

  • Chart of accounts, tax rates and bank accounts you configure once
  • Customer invoices, supplier bills, expenses and payments
  • Budgets tracked against real committed and actual spend
  • Reports built from live data, exportable to CSV whenever you need them

Fleet New

Vehicles and journey management, treated as the controlled activity they are in field operations.

  • Vehicle register with documents, inspections and assigned drivers
  • Journey plans with routes, risk, approval and check-in points
  • Vehicles maintained through the same maintenance module as other assets

Administration

Included with every plan: the controls that decide who is in your organization and what they can do.

  • Invite members by email; they join with the role you chose
  • Build custom roles from the permission registry, tool by tool
  • Switch whole departments or single tools on and off
  • Audit log of membership, role and permission changes
