About CaspianTools

One platform, because the work is one thing

Organizations do not experience themselves as eleven departments. People do the work, equipment supports it, money follows it and safety governs all of it. The software should reflect that.

Why we built it

Field operations end up with a familiar sprawl: an HR system nobody likes, safety in spreadsheets, procurement in an inbox, maintenance in a whiteboard photo, and finance reconciling all of it a month later.

Every one of those tools is defensible on its own. Together they create the real cost: the same person entered four times, a competency that expired without anyone noticing, a purchase order nobody can match to a receipt, and a monthly report that takes a week to assemble and is out of date the day it lands.

Caspian ERP was built the other way round — one multi-tenant platform where the modules share people, items, documents, permissions and reporting from the start. Not an integration project. One system.

What we believe

  • Access control belongs in the database. A hidden button is not a permission.
  • Start with a clear first workflow. Prepare your data, configure access and help your team learn the workflow before expanding.
  • Consistency is a feature. Every list, form and detail page in Caspian ERP behaves the same way, so learning one tool teaches you the rest.
  • Your data is yours. Export it whenever you want, including on the way out.
  • Say what is true. No invented certifications, no roadmap sold as shipped.
