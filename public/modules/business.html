Before CaspianOS

The number on the slide is nobody’s number

By the time a KPI has been exported, pasted, reformatted and presented, it belongs to the person who built the deck rather than to the operation.

Reporting is a monthly project of its own

Someone spends the first week of every month pulling exports together. The reward is a picture of a month that has already finished.

Every department reports a different truth

Procurement counts orders one way, finance counts them another, and the meeting is spent reconciling definitions instead of making decisions.

Project status is a feeling

Progress is described rather than measured, and the first hard evidence of a slip is the deadline itself.

What it does

The layer that turns operations into management information

Two tools, deliberately. This module does not try to be a BI suite — it reads the records the rest of the platform already keeps.

KPIs defined once, measured continuously

Set the target, the owner and the period, and let the value come from the underlying records.

  • Targets with owners, periods and thresholds
  • On-track, at-risk and missed states visible without interpretation
  • History kept, so a trend is a trend and not a memory
  • The same figure everyone sees, because there is only one

A dashboard people actually open

The KPI dashboard is a live view, not a monthly artefact.

  • Current state across every tracked KPI on one screen
  • Drill from a number toward the records behind it
  • Filter to the department or site you are accountable for
  • No export step between the operation and the view

Projects with scope and progress

Projects hold the work, the people and the state — and stay linked to what is happening elsewhere in the platform.

  • Project register with owner, dates and status
  • Detail view holding scope, progress and the team
  • Tasks and meetings attached rather than tracked separately
  • Visible to the people doing the work, not only to the sponsor

Management reporting without the assembly

When the KPI and the record are the same object, the monthly pack stops being a build.

  • Numbers that reconcile with finance because they share a source
  • Period comparisons available without rebuilding a spreadsheet
  • Owners accountable for their own KPIs in the same system they work in
  • Nothing to re-key between the operation and the board pack

Business questions we get asked

Do KPIs update automatically?
They read from the records the rest of the platform keeps, so the figures move as the work does. What you define is the indicator, its target and its owner — not the number itself.
Is this a replacement for a BI tool?
No, and it does not pretend to be. It is the management view of the data already in CaspianOS. If you need modelling across external sources, export and use your BI tool — the value here is that no export is needed for the everyday view.
Can a project span several modules?
That is the normal case. A project usually pulls in tasks and meetings from Efficiency, spend from Finance and people from HR. The project record is where they are seen together.
Who can see the dashboard?
Whoever you allow. KPI visibility is permission-controlled like everything else, so you can publish some indicators organization-wide and restrict others to their owner and the management team.
