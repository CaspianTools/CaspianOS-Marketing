Pricing

Start with your operation.
Let’s discuss pricing.

Tell us about your team, the workflows you want to connect and the support you need to get started. We’ll discuss the scope with you before you make a commitment.

Discuss pricingExplore the modules

Pricing is available on request. Published plans and rates are not yet available.

A useful conversation starts with three things

No need to prepare a full specification. A short description of your operation is enough to start.

01 · Your team

Who will use Caspian?

Tell us your approximate team size, departments and sites, and who needs access to the system.

02 · Your workflows

What needs to connect?

Start with the work that causes the most friction: safety, purchasing, inventory, maintenance or another department.

03 · Your rollout

What will it take to start?

Bring your questions about existing data, approvals, training and support. These help us define a realistic scope.

Before you decide

Pricing questions

Understand the product and the proposed scope before making a purchasing decision.

Where can I find the price list?
We are still developing our pricing model. Contact us to discuss your requirements; there are no published standard rates or plan commitments at this stage.
Can we see the product first?
Yes. Request a demo and tell us which workflows matter to you. You can also read the documentation.
Is pricing per user or per module?
The billing model is not finalized. Share the number of people who need access and the modules you are interested in so we can discuss the right scope.
What about migration and onboarding?
Tell us what systems and spreadsheets you use today. Migration, configuration, training and support requirements need to be discussed alongside the software.
Is there a free trial?
Contact us about evaluation access. Availability, scope, duration and any commercial terms will be confirmed directly before you start.
