Pricing questions

Who counts as a member?
Anyone who holds an active membership in your organization and can sign in. Personnel records, contractor personnel, contacts and vendors are data — they are never charged for. A person you invite but who has not accepted, or whose membership you have suspended, does not count.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. Create your organization and try it with your own data before paying anything. No card is required to start.
Can we change plan later?
Yes, in both directions. Turning a module off keeps its data — it just disappears from the sidebar and from search until you turn it back on.
Do you charge for the modules we don't use?
No. The plan sets which modules are available, and you decide which of those to switch on per department. There is no per-module licence to negotiate.
What about implementation cost?
There is nothing to install or host, so most teams self-implement on Starter or Growth. Enterprise customers who want data migration, custom forms or workflow design get a scoped onboarding — talk to us and we will quote it honestly.
How do we pay?
Annual invoice by bank transfer, or card for smaller plans. Get in touch if your procurement process needs something specific — we would rather fit your process than fight it.
